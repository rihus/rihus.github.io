I am a Research Scientist at the Center for Lung Analytics and Imaging Research (CLAIR),
Division of Pulmonary, Allergy and Critical Care Medicine, University of Alabama at Birmingham.

My background is in physics. During my PhD at the University of Parma (with the Doctor
Europaeus certificate) I used magnetic resonance and related spin-spectroscopy techniques
(NMR/NQR, muon spin spectroscopy (µSR) and neutron scattering) to study materials for quantum
information processing, data storage and energy conversion. I then moved into biomedical
MRI, working with clinical and preclinical scanners.

In recent years my work has centred on hyperpolarized ¹²⁹Xe lung MRI: faster and more accurate
acquisition (spiral imaging, flip-angle and B₁ corrections, advanced reconstruction),
robust quantification of lung ventilation, and applying these methods in clinical research
studies, including people with cystic fibrosis receiving modulator therapy.

I am an MRI physicist who develops and validates imaging methods that measure how
the lungs work. My focus is hyperpolarized ¹²⁹Xe lung MRI — from pulse sequence design
and image reconstruction on 3T and low-field 0.55T Siemens scanners through to the
quantitative analysis that turns images into clinically meaningful numbers, such as
ventilation defect percentage in people with cystic fibrosis.

I trained as a physicist in magnetic resonance and spin spectroscopy (PhD, University of
Parma), and I am currently a Research Scientist at the University of Alabama at Birmingham.

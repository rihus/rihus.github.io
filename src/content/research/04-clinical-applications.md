Cystic fibrosis (CF) is a genetic condition in which thick mucus can block the small
airways. Newer **highly effective modulator therapies** have greatly improved lung health
for many people with CF, which makes small remaining changes harder to detect with
standard breathing tests.

Because xenon MRI shows ventilation region by region, it can pick up changes that
whole-lung tests may miss. Our work showed that xenon MRI detects **ongoing ventilation
improvements** in people with CF receiving highly effective modulator therapy. I have also
contributed to studies of healthy ventilation across the lifespan and of whether the
menstrual cycle influences xenon gas-exchange MRI measurements in healthy women — the kind
of groundwork needed before an imaging method can be used reliably in clinical trials.

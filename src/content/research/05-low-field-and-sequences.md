A **pulse sequence** is the precise recipe of radio-frequency pulses and magnetic-field
gradients that an MRI scanner plays out to create an image. Changing that recipe changes
what the image shows, how long it takes and how good it looks.

I develop pulse sequences on Siemens **3T** and **low-field 0.55T** scanners. Low-field
MRI systems can be more affordable and more comfortable for patients, and they are
particularly interesting for lung imaging, but protocols designed for standard high-field
scanners usually need to be re-thought before they work well at lower field. I also build
open, vendor-neutral sequences with Pulseq (see [Projects](/projects/)).
